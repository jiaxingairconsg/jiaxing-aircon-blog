Company · Jiaxing Aircon

One Aircon Company for Every Stage of Your Cooling Needs in Singapore

From setting up your first air conditioning system to maintaining the units you rely on every day, having a dependable service provider makes a difference. Established in 2014, Jiaxing Holdings Pte. Ltd. brings more than a decade of experience to residential and commercial customers across Singapore. With over 50 professionals and a fleet of 38 fully equipped service vans, we are well positioned to support homeowners and businesses with their everyday and unexpected air conditioning needs.

Our comprehensive aircon servicing in Singapore includes one-time fan coil unit cleaning, scheduled maintenance, aircon chemical wash and overhaul, fault diagnosis, circuit board repairs and new aircon installation. Whether your unit is leaking water, needs a deeper clean or is no longer performing as expected, our technicians assess its condition and recommend an appropriate solution. We also install systems from trusted brands, including Daikin, Mitsubishi and Panasonic, and offer maintenance arrangements for customers who prefer planned servicing throughout the year.

At Jiaxing, our goal goes beyond restoring cold air. We want customers to feel confident about the condition of their air conditioning systems and the support available when they need it. Our commitment to quality, experienced technicians and transparent service packages helps make routine maintenance and unexpected repairs easier to manage.
